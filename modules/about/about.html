ExpenseFlow – Your Personal Money Companion

ExpenseFlow helps you see where your money comes from and where it goes. Record your daily income and spending, set limits for yourself, and get a clear picture of your finances every month — all in one simple place.

  • Made forStudents, young professionals and anyone who wants to manage money better
  • Your dataSaved privately on this device only
  • CostFree to use

Track Every Peso

Log your income and expenses in seconds. Add a title, amount, category and payment method so you always know what each record is for.

Stay Within Budget

Set a monthly spending limit or limits per category. ExpenseFlow warns you when you are getting close so you can adjust before you overspend.

Understand Your Habits

Easy-to-read charts and reports show your top spending, monthly trends and helpful tips so you can make smarter money decisions.

How to Get Started

  1. Sign in with your username and password
  2. Check your dashboard overview
  3. Add your first income or expense
  4. Organize records with categories
  5. Set a monthly budget
  6. Add repeating bills or allowances
  7. Review your reports
  8. Back up your records anytime
  9. Keep building better money habits
