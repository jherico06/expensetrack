Guide 1

Getting Started & Your Local Account

How account creation, authentication, and your profile work.

1
Create Your Account

On the landing page, switch to the Register tab. Enter your Display Name, a unique Username, and Password. Your account is created locally on your device with no third-party servers.

2
Configure Your Currency in Profile

Click your avatar or name at the bottom of the sidebar to visit your Profile. Choose your preferred currency (₱ PHP, $ USD, € EUR, £ GBP, or ¥ JPY). All calculations immediately adapt.

3
Stay Signed In Locally

Your session stays active across page reloads. Whenever you wish to switch users or lock your session, click Log Out from the sidebar or Profile banner.

Open My Profile →
Guide 2

Recording & Managing Entries

Logging expenses and income, applying filters, and editing records.

1
Click "+ Add Entry"

Navigate to Entries in the sidebar, or click the yellow + Add Entry button in the header toolbar to open the entry creation window.

2
Choose Type: Expense or Income

Select Expense (money spent) or Income (money earned). Select a relevant category (Food, Salary, Bills, etc.) and enter the amount.

3
Specify Payment Method, Date & Notes

Pick how it was paid (Cash, Bank Transfer, GCash / E-Wallet, Credit Card), the date of the record, and optional notes (e.g., store name, receipt reference).

4
Filter, Search & Sort Entries

Use the comprehensive filters in the Entries module to search by keyword, filter by category, payment method, date range, or amount limits. Sort by newest, highest, or lowest.

5
Edit, Duplicate or Delete

Click the three dots (•••) on any entry row to view full details, duplicate the entry to save time, edit its details, or delete it.

Go to Entries →
Guide 3

Setting Monthly & Category Budgets

Stay disciplined with automated progress bars and threshold warnings.

1
Set an Overall Monthly Budget

Go to the Budgets module. Click Set Monthly Budget and enter your target maximum spending for the current month (e.g. ₱20,000).

2
Add Category-Specific Limits

Click + Add Category Budget to place limits on high-spending categories like Food & Dining, Shopping, or Transportation.

3
Understand Color-Coded Budget Alerts

Watch the progress meter update in real time with each logged expense:

  • Safe (0% – 79%): Green bar — you are well within your spending target.
  • Warning (80% – 99%): Amber bar — you are approaching your limit.
  • Exceeded (≥100%): Red bar — over-budget! An alert is triggered in the notification bell.
Configure Budgets →
Guide 4

Organizing, Adding & Deleting Categories

Customize how your spending and earnings are grouped.

1
Browse Active Categories

Visit Categories in the sidebar. Switch between Expenses and Income tabs to see all current classifications with their icons and usage counts.

2
Add a Custom Category

Click + New Category. Enter a descriptive title (e.g., "Gym & Fitness", "Pet Care", "Side Hustle"), choose whether it is an Expense or Income, and select one of 24 Lucide icons.

3
Edit or Delete Any Category

Every category can be edited or deleted. Click the pencil icon to rename or update its icon, or click the trash icon to remove it. When deleted, associated records are safely tagged as "Other".

Manage Categories →
Guide 5

Automating Recurring Expenses & Bills

Never miss a recurring subscription, rent, or scheduled allowance.

1
Create a Recurring Rule

In the Recurring module, click + New Recurring. Give it a name (e.g., "Apartment Rent", "Spotify", "Monthly Allowance"), select the category, amount, and frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly).

2
Pause or Resume Subscriptions

Toggle the status switch on any recurring item between Active and Paused. Paused subscriptions won't trigger due alerts.

3
1-Click Convert to Logged Entry

When a recurring expense is due, ExpenseFlow highlights it. Click the play button (Log Entry) to automatically record it as an active entry for today's date without retyping details.

Open Recurring →

Guide 7

Data Privacy, CSV Export & JSON Backups

Keep your financial records safe and portable forever.

1
100% Local Storage Architecture

Your financial entries, budgets, and categories never leave your computer. There are no cloud servers, no databases, and no trackers looking at your finances.

2
Download Regular Backups

Visit Settings or Profile and click Export JSON (or Back Up My Records). Save the generated file to your Documents or cloud drive once a week.

3
Transfer to Another Computer

To transfer your data to a new laptop, copy your saved JSON backup file. On the new laptop, open ExpenseFlow, click Import JSON in Settings, and choose the file. All your records will be restored instantly.

Go to Settings →

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to the most common questions about ExpenseFlow.

Privacy Where is my financial data stored? Can anyone else see it?

100% on your device. All your financial entries, categories, budgets, and profile details are saved inside your web browser's local storage (localStorage).

ExpenseFlow does not have a central database server, does not connect to external financial institutions, and has zero advertising or analytics trackers. Nobody except someone sitting directly in front of your unlocked browser can view your records.

Data Safety What happens if I clear my browser history, cache, or cookies?

If you click "Clear all website data" in your browser settings, the browser will erase all local storage keys, which will reset ExpenseFlow.

How to protect your records: We strongly advise using the Back Up My Records button (in Profile or Settings) to export a .json backup to your computer or Google Drive. Restoring from a backup takes less than 5 seconds!

Entries What is the difference between an Expense and an Income entry?

An Expense represents money leaving your wallet or bank account (such as groceries, tuition, bills, transportation). Expenses decrease your available balance and count against your active budgets.

An Income represents money entering your balance (such as salary, monthly allowance, freelancing, gifts). Income increases your available balance and is highlighted in vibrant green.

Categories Can I edit, delete, or add new categories?

Yes, absolutely! ExpenseFlow provides complete CRUD (Create, Read, Update, Delete) control over all categories.

  • To add: click + New Category in the Categories module and choose a name, type, and icon.
  • To edit: click the pencil icon on any category card to change its title or icon.
  • To delete: click the trash button. If any existing entries were using that category, they are safely preserved and reassigned to "Other".
Budgets How do the budget warnings and alerts work?

ExpenseFlow monitors your total spending against your set budget limits:

  • Under 80%: Displayed in green, indicating you are on track.
  • 80% to 99%: Marked with an amber warning badge to caution you that you are nearing your ceiling.
  • 100% or above: The bar turns solid red, and an immediate notification badge is added to the bell icon in the top header.
Recurring How do recurring expenses work? Does it automatically charge my account?

ExpenseFlow is a tracker and ledger, not a payment gateway, so it does not pull money from your bank account or card.

Instead, the Recurring module tracks upcoming bills (like rent, phone bills, tuition, or Netflix) and calculates next due dates. When a bill is due, a badge alert notifies you, and with a single click of the Log Entry play button, ExpenseFlow logs that transaction into your Entries table.

Profile How do I change my currency from Philippine Peso (PHP) to USD or EUR?

Click your user profile in the bottom of the sidebar or navigate to #/profile. In the Preferred Currency dropdown, select your desired currency (PHP ₱, USD $, EUR €, GBP £, or JPY ¥) and click Update Profile. All amounts across your dashboard, tables, and reports will instantly format with your chosen currency symbol.

Export Can I export my data to Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets?

Yes! Go to the Settings module and click Export CSV. A clean, formatted .csv spreadsheet containing every recorded entry (Date, Title, Type, Category, Amount, Payment Method, and Notes) will download immediately for opening in Excel, Apple Numbers, or Google Sheets.

Multi-User Can multiple people use ExpenseFlow on the same computer?

Yes. If you share a family or school computer, you can click Log Out. Another person can register their own unique username and password. Each user gets their own personalized greeting and session credentials.

Reset How do I start completely fresh with zero entries?

Go to Settings → Data Management → click Reset All Data. You will be prompted with a confirmation dialog. Confirming will reset your ledger, budgets, and categories back to clean defaults.
